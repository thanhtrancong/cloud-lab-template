DEPLOYMENT STEPS
================
1. Update ~/.lab07.env with your config
2. Run: bash scripts/setup.sh
3. Run: wrangler login
4. Update wrangler.toml with your domain
5. Run: wrangler deploy
6. Test: bash scripts/test.sh
7. Wait for DNS propagation
8. Test domain: curl https://api.vlsc07-nguyenvana.site/

TESTING COMMANDS
